Dual Reader — Bilingual Ebook Reader

Read any book in two languages, side by side.

Dual Reader is a bilingual ebook reader that makes reading foreign-language books effortless. Import any EPUB file and read the original text alongside an AI-powered translation — no dictionaries needed, no tab-switching, just immersive reading.

Key Features:

📖 Split-Screen Reading
Read the original text and your target language side by side. Vertical layout on phones, side-by-side on tablets and landscape mode.

🌍 AI-Powered Translation
Tap any page to get an instant, context-aware translation. Our AI understands the full book context for natural, accurate translations — not word-by-word machine output.

📚 Your Library, Organized
Import EPUB files from your device. Track reading progress, add bookmarks with notes, and search through text. Your library is always ready.

🎨 Beautiful Themes
Choose from 7 carefully crafted themes including Night Mode with true black for OLED screens, Sepia for warm reading, Ocean, Forest, and more.

🔤 Fully Customizable
Adjust font size, line height, and reading preferences. Toggle sentence numbers for language learning.

📱 Works Offline
On-device translation fallback via ML Kit means you can keep reading even without internet.

🔒 Privacy First
No accounts required. No tracking. Your books stay on your device. Translation text is processed securely and not stored permanently.

• Bilingual readers who enjoy books in two languages
• Students studying foreign literature
• Anyone who wants to read books in a language they're learning
