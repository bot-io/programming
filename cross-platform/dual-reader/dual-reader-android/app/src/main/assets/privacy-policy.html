Privacy Policy for Dual Reader

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Overview

Dual Reader is an Android application that lets you read bilingual ebooks. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Dual Reader operates entirely on your device.

Book Files

EPUB files you import are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to any server. You can delete them at any time by removing the books from the app or uninstalling the app.

Translation

To translate text, Dual Reader sends the text content of your book pages to our translation service. This data is processed solely to provide translations and is:

  • Not stored permanently on our servers
  • Not linked to you personally (no accounts, no tracking identifiers)
  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)

A per-device daily quota limits translation usage. The device identifier is a random UUID generated on first install — it cannot identify you.

Translation Cache

Translated text may be cached on our servers to improve performance for other users reading the same book. This cache contains only book text and its translation — no personal data.

Local Storage

Dual Reader stores the following locally on your device:

  • Imported EPUB files
  • Reading progress and bookmarks
  • Translation cache (translated pages)
  • App settings (theme, font size, language preferences)

All local data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

Dual Reader uses the following third-party services:

  • Google ML Kit — On-device translation fallback. No data is sent to Google when using ML Kit.
  • Cloudflare Workers — Our translation proxy server. See "Translation" above.

Children's Privacy

Dual Reader does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
