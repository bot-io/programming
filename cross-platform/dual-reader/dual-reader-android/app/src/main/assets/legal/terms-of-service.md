# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 18, 2026**

Dual Reader ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our app.

## 1. What We Collect

**We do NOT collect personal data.** Dual Reader does not require an account, email address, or any personal information to use.

The only identifier we use is:
- A **random installation ID** (auto-generated, non-personal) used solely for fair usage rate-limiting on our free translation proxy.

## 2. How Translation Works

When you translate text using cloud translation:
- The **text of the book page** you are reading is sent to our translation proxy
- The proxy forwards it to AI translation providers (Google Gemini or GLM/Z.AI)
- The translation result is returned to your device
- Translations are **cached locally** on your device for offline access

**Your book content is never stored on our servers permanently.** Translation cache on the proxy is temporary and keyed by text hash, not by user identity.

When using **on-device translation** (ML Kit models): no data leaves your device.

## 3. Data Storage

All of the following are stored **locally on your device only**:
- Your book library and imported EPUB files
- Reading position, bookmarks, and settings
- Cached translations
- Downloaded language models

We have no servers storing your personal reading data.

## 4. Billing (Google Play)

If you purchase a subscription or one-time unlock:
- Payment is processed by **Google Play Billing** — we never see your payment information
- Google shares your purchase entitlement status with us to unlock features
- Purchase data is managed by Google according to their privacy policy

## 5. Third-Party Services

- **Google Gemini API**: Processes text for cloud translation
- **GLM/Z.AI API**: Alternative translation provider
- **Google ML Kit**: On-device translation (no data sent to servers)
- **Google Play Billing**: Payment processing

## 6. Children's Privacy

Dual Reader is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 7. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. You can clear all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted within the app and on our website.

## 9. Contact

Questions? Email: support@dualreader.app
