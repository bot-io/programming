**Bug 1 — Double-gate problem (the main one you noticed):**

**Fix:** Removed the probabilistic gate entirely. Now when the cooldown expires and the particle has enough energy, it reproduces *immediately*. The cooldown alone controls the rate, exactly as the `reproductionCooldownSec` setting implies.

Energy was deducted and cooldown was reset *before* calling `spawn()`. If the spawn failed (e.g. at population cap), the parent lost energy AND got a full cooldown — burning a reproduction cycle for nothing.
