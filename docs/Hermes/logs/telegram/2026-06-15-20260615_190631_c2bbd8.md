**🔴 Population cap crash (the real cause):**
The `SpatialHashGrid` has a `next: Int32Array(maxParticles)` for its linked list. When you increased the cap, `rebuildSimulation()` created a new bigger `EcosystemWorld` but the grid was **never recreated** — the condition `eco.config.populationCap !== liveConfig.populationCap` was always `false` because `eco` was just built from `liveConfig`. So the grid's `next` array stayed at the old size. Particles spawning at indices beyond the old cap corrupted the grid, and queries reading garbage `next` pointers caused the crash.

**Fix:** Added `grid.maxParticles` field to `SpatialHashGrid`, changed the comparison to `grid.maxParticles < liveConfig.populationCap`.
