### 1. Dynamic "Add/Remove Force" never updates the UI — stale indices control wrong forces
**Files:** `main.ts:1148-1158`, `controls.ts:529-584`

- Added forces never appear in the panel (user can't tune them).
- `removeForce` uses `splice(index, 1)` (`main.ts:559`), which shifts every subsequent force's index down by one — but each rendered force row retains its original `index` (baked into the click handlers in `makeForceRow`, `controls.ts:550,566,577`). After deleting force #1, the row labelled "force 2" now silently controls force #1's instance, and its delete button calls `onRemoveForce(2)` which is out of range → no-op. **Fix:** Give `createControlsPanel` (or a returned handle) a `rebuildForcesSection(pipeline, descriptors)` method and call it from `onAddForce` / `onRemoveForce`. Stable force *ids* (not positional indices) should back the handlers.

### 2. Population Cap slider triggers a full `rebuildSimulation()` on every drag pixel
**Files:** `controls.ts:412` (`makeSlider` binds `input`), `controls.ts:708-720` (Pop Cap slider), `main.ts:1128-1131`
**Performance bug.** `makeSlider` fires on the continuous `input` event. `onPopulationCapChange` immediately calls `rebuildSimulation()`, which recreates the entire ecosystem, spatial grid, interaction matrix, logger, and resets the renderer. Dragging the Pop Cap slider from 50→2000 destroys and rebuilds the world dozens of times per second → severe jank, particle reset storms, and log spam.
**Fix:** Debounce `onPopulationCapChange` (e.g. 250 ms trailing) or bind this slider to the `change` event (fires on release) instead of `input`.
