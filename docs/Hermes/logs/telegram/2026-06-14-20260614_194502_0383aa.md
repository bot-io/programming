You were right — the reproduction logic had two real bugs:

**Bug 1: Double-gate problem**
After a particle's cooldown expired, it still had to pass a probabilistic RNG check (only 0.2% chance per tick at 60fps). This meant the *actual* average reproduction interval was ~2× the configured cooldown. A predator with `reproductionCooldownSec: 8` actually took ~16 seconds on average between reproductions — most of its 60s lifespan.

**Bug 2: Energy wasted on failed spawn**

**Fix:** Spawn the child first. Only deduct energy and reset cooldown if spawn actually succeeds.
