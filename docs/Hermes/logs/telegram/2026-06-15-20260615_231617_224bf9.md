## 🧲 Proposed: Multi-Zone Force Model

**Current system:** Each species pair (i,j) has ONE `InteractionEntry` — a single `strength` (attract/repel), single `radius`, single `falloff`. You can't say "repel when close, attract when far."

```
Zone 2: [20px – 80px]  strength: +0.6  (attract — bonding range)  
Zone 3: [80px – 150px] strength: -0.1  (weak repel — spacing)
```
