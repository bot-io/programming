In the **Controls Panel → Interaction Matrix** section, you get an N×N grid where each cell represents the force from species A (row) → species B (column).

**Tap any cell** to open the editor with 4 sliders:

- **Inner Strength** — force magnitude at close range (`0` to `innerRadius`)
- **Outer Strength** — force magnitude at detection range (`innerRadius` to `outerRadius`)
- **Inner Radius** — boundary between the two zones
- **Outer Radius** — maximum range; no force beyond this
- **Falloff** dropdown — how force decays within a zone (linear, inverse, constant)

**How the zones work:**
- Distance `< innerRadius` → applies **innerStrength** (e.g. predator lunges hard at close range)
- Distance `innerRadius` to `outerRadius` → applies **outerStrength** (e.g. weak attraction from far away to detect prey)
- Distance `≥ outerRadius` → no force

**Typical predator setup:** large outerRadius + weak positive outerStrength (detect prey from far) + small innerRadius + strong positive innerStrength (lunge when close).

**Typical prey setup:** inverse — flee with negative strength, larger detection radius.

There's also a **Randomize** button that fills the matrix with seeded random values.
