Fixed. Here's what was wrong and what changed:

**Root cause — discontinuous zone boundary**

The old formula had a hard switch at `innerRadius`:
- Inner zone faded to **0** at innerRadius
- Outer zone started at **full** outerStrength at innerRadius

This created a force "cliff" that yanked every particle pair to exactly `innerRadius` distance → rigid ring patterns.

**New formula — smooth blended transition**

Inside the inner zone (0 → innerRadius), repulsion and attraction are **smoothly blended** using smoothstep:
- At d=0: pure innerStrength (full repulsion)
- At d=innerRadius/2: weighted blend (equilibrium where they cancel)
- At d=innerRadius: pure outerStrength (full attraction)

In the outer zone (innerRadius → outerRadius): attraction linearly decays to 0.

This gives **exactly one equilibrium** — where repulsion and attraction naturally balance — instead of locking particles into artificial ring distances. Particles should now form organic clusters/flocks instead of circles.

**Note on your config:** `innerRadius: 85` is very large for 120 particles in 725×320. Each particle wants 85px of personal space but there's only ~40px² available per particle. Try reducing innerRadius to ~20-30 and you'll get tighter, more natural flocking.
